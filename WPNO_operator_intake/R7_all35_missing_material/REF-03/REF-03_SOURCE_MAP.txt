REF-03 SOURCE MAP

1. REF-03_ISO_IEC_7064_2003.pdf

Role:
Identifies ISO/IEC 7064:2003 and establishes that MOD 11,10 is a hybrid
numeric system with one check digit and designation 6.

Limitation:
This is an iTeh preview. It contains normative pages 1–5 only and does not
contain clauses 9 and 10.

It must not be described as the complete ISO standard.

2. REF-03_ELSTER_Pruefung_Steueridentifikationsnummer_2026-04-15.pdf

Authority:
Bayerisches Landesamt für Steuern / ELSTER.

Role:
Official German tax-administration specification of the IdNr structure,
official examples and exact check-digit calculation.

Relevant location:
Section 2.2, especially document pages 6–8.

3. REF-03_BZSt_German_IdNr_Issuer_Specification.pdf

Role:
Official background evidence that the German IdNr consists of eleven digits
and that the eleventh digit is the check digit.

4. Independent implementation evidence

python-stdnum 2.2:
stdnum/de/idnr.py
stdnum/iso7064/mod_11_10.py

Role:
Independent executable implementation and test-vector support.

CONCLUSION:

The ISO preview is not treated as a complete standard.

The missing operational MOD 11,10 calculation is supplied by the official
ELSTER specification and independently corroborated by python-stdnum 2.2.
